What is a frost-free hose bib and do I need one?

A frost-free hose bib (sillcock) shuts the water off inside the heated part of the wall, so the outdoor faucet is much less likely to freeze and split. In Minnesota they are worth it, especially if an old outdoor faucet has already cracked.
